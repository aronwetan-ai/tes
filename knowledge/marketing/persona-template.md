# Persona Template

Versi: 1.0 (Update 11)
Last updated: 2026-05-17
Audience: BrandFlow agents — `@brandflow.cmo`, `@brandflow.copywriter`, `@brandflow.analytics`, `@brandflow.research`

---

## Why This Template

Junior personas: age + gender + city + job title. Useless — everyone in the segment looks identical.

Senior personas: 3 layers (demographic → psychographic → behavioral). Layer 3 is where copy lands. Without Layer 3, the persona is fiction.

This file is the **canonical agency template** used at client onboarding and per-campaign brief refinement.

---

## The 3-Layer Structure

### Layer 1 — Demographic (basic identification)

Necessary, not sufficient. Used for filtering audiences in ads platforms.

- Age range (e.g. 28-38)
- Location (city / region / country)
- Role / job title (specific: "Head of Marketing at SaaS startup," not "marketer")
- Company size (employees / revenue band)
- Income range
- Education / professional credentials
- Family status (where relevant)

### Layer 2 — Psychographic (what they care about)

The persona's interior life. Drives messaging tone + positioning.

- **Goals** (next 6 months — concrete, time-boxed)
- **Fears** (what's keeping them up at night — specific, not "uncertainty")
- **Identity statements** ("I'm the kind of person who…")
- **Status concerns** (peer perception they care about, in their context)
- **Trusted voices** (whose content they consume — specific people / publications)
- **Aspirations** (who/what they want to be 2-5 years out)
- **Self-narrative** (the story they tell themselves about why they're here)

### Layer 3 — Behavioral (what they actually do)

The action layer. Drives where to reach them and what language to use.

- **Where they spend attention** (specific platforms, hours per week, peak times)
- **How they research before buying** (search vs ask peers vs read newsletter vs YouTube)
- **What triggered past buy/sign-up decisions** (specific past purchases + the trigger)
- **Objections that stopped past purchases** (specific times they almost bought but didn't, why)
- **Words they use for their own problem** (their language, not industry jargon — collected verbatim from interviews / DMs / reviews)
- **Decision speed** (impulse / 1-2 week / 1-3 month / >6 month)
- **Influence on decision** (sole / influenced by partner / committee / boss)
- **Tools they currently use** (incumbents you compete with or complement)
- **Communities they're in** (Slack / Discord / FB group / industry email list)

## Source Hierarchy (Higher → Lower Quality)

| Rank | Source | Quality | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1:1 conversations with 5-7 actual customers | Highest | High (1-2 weeks scheduling) |
| 2 | Sales / support transcripts | High | Low |
| 3 | Customer reviews (theirs + competitors') | High | Low |
| 4 | Social listening on hashtags + relevant communities | Medium | Low |
| 5 | Search query mining (Answer the Public, Reddit, Quora) | Medium | Low |
| 6 | Surveys (claimed preferences) | Low-Medium | Medium |
| 7 | Pure demographic data | Low | Low |

Senior workflow: blend top 3 into the persona; treat 4-5 as triangulation; rely on 6-7 only when nothing better exists.

---

## How Many Personas Per Client

For agency-context UMKM / personal-brand clients:

- **1-2 primary personas** for most clients (don't go beyond this).
- **3 max** for clients with genuinely distinct audience segments (rare).
- **Never** the cliché "we have 7 personas" deck — unactionable.

If two segments share Layer 3 verbatim language patterns, they're one persona with sub-variants, not two personas.

---

## Refresh Cadence

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Quarterly | Light refresh: re-run social listening, refresh top phrases, note shifts |
| New segment lights up in analytics | Investigate — possible new persona forming |
| Engagement on top performer drops 30%+ | Audit — persona may have shifted or content may have drifted from persona |
| Client pivot / new product | Full re-build |
| Major macro shift (recession, tech wave) | Re-validate fears + status concerns specifically |

A persona that hasn't been refreshed in 6+ months is suspect. Marketing trends move fast.

---

## Common Persona Failures

- **Pure demographic.** Age + city + role = 0 actionable info.
- **Inferred fears with no source.** "She's worried about scaling" — based on what?
- **Aspirational paint.** Persona reads like LinkedIn highlight reel; doesn't match real customer state.
- **Too many.** 7 personas = 0 used in production. Cap at 1-3.
- **Static.** Built once, never updated, used for years.
- **No verbatim language.** Layer 3 missing → all copy fails the "this is for me" test.
- **Mixing real customers with target customers.** Be explicit which the persona represents.
