## Why This File

A hook is the first 1-3 lines (or 1.5 seconds for video) that decide whether the rest of the piece is read. Hooks are pickable, not inventable from scratch — there are roughly 7 mechanisms and ~30 patterns in steady use across modern marketing copy.

This file is the **reference taxonomy**. The pickable agency-context library lives at `companies/brandflow/skills/content/hook-library.md`; this file explains the underlying mechanisms.

---

## What a Hook Is Doing

A hook does ONE of three jobs:

1. **STOP** the scroll / earn the open / earn the click.
2. **HOLD** the audience already reading — keep them past line 2.
3. **TELL** in a way that makes the reader want to know what comes next.

Different funnel positions need different jobs:
- TOFU (cold audience): STOP-dominant.
- MOFU (warm): HOLD-dominant with mild STOP.
- BOFU (hot): TELL-dominant — direct, fact-led, specific.

## The 7 Mechanisms

### 1. Curiosity Gap

Open a gap between expectation and reveal; the reader keeps reading to close it.

**Triggers:** "I thought X, turns out Y." / "The thing nobody tells you about X." / "Why X always Y."

**Best for:** TOFU, education content, awareness campaigns.

**Fails when:** the gap is too big (reader doesn't trust the eventual close) or too small (no real reveal).

### 2. Pain Mirror

Reflect the audience's frustration back at them in their own words.

**Triggers:** "Pernah X? Kamu tidak sendirian." / "Setiap [audience] yang scaling pasti hit dinding ini."

**Best for:** MOFU, problem-led content, empathy-driven campaigns.

**Fails when:** the pain is generic ("it's hard out there") or when the audience doesn't actually feel the pain you're mirroring.

### 3. Contrast / Comparison

Set X against Y to create stakes.

**Triggers:** "Cara lama: X. Cara sekarang: Y." / "Most do X. We do Y." / "Before: A. After: B."

**Best for:** TOFU/MOFU, repositioning content, before/after stories.

**Fails when:** the contrast is artificial, or when X (the rejected option) is a strawman.

### 4. Authority / Receipt

Signal credibility immediately so the reader gives you the benefit of the doubt.

**Triggers:** "Setelah 100 launch, satu pattern muncul:" / "Klien yang tutup deal Rp 500jt minggu lalu pakai pattern ini:"

**Best for:** MOFU/BOFU, sales-leaning content, trust-building.

**Fails when:** the receipt is unspecific ("we've been doing this for years") or when authority is borrowed without earning it.

### 5. Specificity

Hyper-specific hooks make the reader feel "this is for me."

**Triggers:** "Buat founder SaaS B2B Indonesia yang baru raise seed:" / "5 hal yang kami pelajari dari 100 carousel di Q1 2026."

**Best for:** any funnel stage. Universally strong.

**Fails when:** not actually specific ("for everyone who wants to grow") — that's L1 specificity, not real.

### 6. Bold Claim

Force a stance that the reader has to react to (agree, disagree, want to know more).

**Triggers:** "Hashtag ≠ jangkauan." / "Cold email balik bukan karena copy, tapi karena timing." / "Brand awareness < brand recognition."

**Best for:** TOFU, opinion-leader content, contrarian threads.

**Fails when:** the claim is provocation without substance, or when the body doesn't back it.

### 7. Story Open

Open a narrative loop the body closes.

**Triggers:** "Tahun lalu klien kami hampir tutup." / "Senin pagi, 09:14, client DM 'urgent'." / "'Kontennya bagus, tapi kok ga jualan?' — kalimat yang ngubah retainer kami."

**Best for:** brand-building content, long-form, founder-led posts.

**Fails when:** the story is fictional / lacks specificity, or when the loop never closes.

## Length / Format Constraints

| Surface | Hook real estate | Optimal length |
|---|---|---|
| IG feed caption | ~125-140 chars before "more" | 1 line, 8-12 words ideal |
| LinkedIn feed | ~150-200 chars before "see more" | 1-2 lines |
| X / Twitter standalone | 280 chars total | 1 sentence (sometimes 2) |
| TikTok / Reels visual hook | 1.5 seconds | 3-7 word on-screen + audio hook |
| Email subject | 35-50 chars (mobile preview) | very tight |
| Email preheader | 50-90 chars | extends subject |
| Blog title (SEO) | 50-60 chars | tight + keyword + benefit |
| YouTube title | ~60 chars before truncation | tight + emotional anchor |
| Push notification | ~40-65 chars | very tight + curiosity + relevance |

## Construction Rules

1. **One mechanism per hook.** Stacking ("contrarian + authority + curiosity") dilutes.
2. **Earn the second line.** Hook's job is to make reader want line 2 — line 2 must deliver.
3. **Specific > vague every time.** Climb the specificity ladder until the line stops sounding stock.
4. **Verb-first or pronoun-first openings outperform article-first.** "Saya" / "Cara" / "Setiap" > "Sebuah" / "Adalah".
5. **No hype superlatives.** "GAME-CHANGING," "REVOLUTIONARY," "VIRAL" — invisible the moment they land.
6. **No question-no-payoff.** Question hooks need an implied "answer is below."
7. **Mobile-first reading.** Most hooks are read on a 6-inch screen, vertical, in noisy environments. Test there first.

## Stress-Test Before Submitting

```
[ ] Can the hook stand alone? (Hide the body — does it still earn a read?)
[ ] Is the mechanism nameable? (If not, you're hoping, not picking.)
[ ] Does it climb the specificity ladder past L2?
[ ] Does the body deliver what the hook implies?
[ ] Is it on-archetype for this brand?
[ ] Does it land in the channel's hook real estate (length-wise)?
[ ] Does it match the funnel stage?
[ ] Does it feel native to the channel's grammar?
```

If 6+ of 8 are yes, ship. If <5, revise before submitting to QA.

## Hook Anti-Patterns

- **Stock openers.** "In a sea of content..." / "In today's fast-paced world..." / "Let me tell you something."
- **Question-no-payoff.** "Pernah merasa stuck?" with no implied unstuck.
- **Faux personal.** "Klien saya kemarin..." with no name, role, or specifics. Smells fictional.
- **Hyperbole.** "GAME-CHANGING," "100% guaranteed," "the best in Indonesia."
- **Click-bait.** Hook promises X; body delivers Y. Reader feels tricked.
- **All-caps.** Reads desperate; often triggers algorithmic suppression.
- **Three emoji as substitute for clarity.** ✨🚀💡 doesn't make a weak hook strong.
- **Pun-as-hook.** Wordplay rarely communicates clearly; ages badly.
