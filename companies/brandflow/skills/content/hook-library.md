# Hook Library — BrandFlow Deep Skill

Agent-specific extension of `skills/content/SKILL.md`. Used by `@brandflow.copywriter` whenever a piece needs a hook — which is every piece. Hook selection is a senior judgment call; this file pins the shortcuts.

Core principle: **hooks are picked, not invented from scratch.** Stop inventing. Pick from the library, calibrate to brand + audience + channel + funnel stage.

---

## When to Use

- Drafting any caption, ad copy, email subject, blog title, video script intro.
- Pushing back on weak hooks during QA.
- Onboarding a new copywriter / freelancer to the agency's house method.
- Creating a hook bank for a new client (3-5 brand-fit patterns).

---

## Hook Anatomy

A hook is **one mechanical decision + one specific phrase**. The mechanism creates the read; the phrase carries the brand voice.

```
[MECHANISM]   What it triggers in the reader's brain (curiosity / pain / contrast / etc.)
[PHRASE]      The specific words that carry the mechanism + the client's voice
```

If you can name the mechanism, you can swap the phrase per client without losing the read. If you can't name the mechanism, you're hoping.

## The Library — 30 Patterns by Mechanism

### Curiosity Mechanism (gap between expectation and reveal)

| ID | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| C-1 | Reversed expectation | "Saya kira X. Ternyata Y." |
| C-2 | Counterintuitive number | "Klien terbaik kami bukan yang paling banyak bayar." |
| C-3 | Hidden cost | "Yang tidak ada di brosur agensi mana pun:" |
| C-4 | Insider angle | "Hal yang kami baru tahu setelah 100 campaign:" |
| C-5 | Question with payoff promise | "Kenapa cold email yang panjang lebih sering balas? Begini." |

### Pain Mechanism (mirror the reader's frustration)

| ID | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| P-1 | Specific pain ack | "Posting tiap hari, engagement-nya stuck. Saya pernah di situ." |
| P-2 | Cost-of-status-quo | "Setiap minggu konten 'meh' = follower hilang yang tidak balik." |
| P-3 | Permission to feel it | "Boleh capek kelola 5 client account sendirian." |
| P-4 | Universalize the niche | "Setiap founder yang scaling pasti hit dinding ini." |

### Contrast Mechanism (X vs Y, before/after, them/us)

| ID | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| K-1 | Before/After | "Engagement 0.8% sebelum. 4.2% sesudah. Ini yang berubah:" |
| K-2 | Common-vs-better | "Most agencies ship 4 posts/week. We ship 3 posts that compound." |
| K-3 | Old-vs-new playbook | "Cara lama: spray 20 hashtag. Cara sekarang: 5 yang pas." |
| K-4 | What they think vs what works | "Kamu kira engagement = like. Algoritma 2026 baca save." |

### Authority Mechanism (signal credibility upfront)

| ID | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A-1 | Specific receipt | "Setelah 1.2M IDR di Meta Ads minggu lalu, ini yang kami pelajari:" |
| A-2 | Insider citation | "Pakai pattern ini di brief untuk 8 dari 10 retainer kami." |
| A-3 | Failed-then-learned | "Tahun lalu kami gagal 3 launch. Pattern yang berubah:" |
| A-4 | Borrowed authority | "Kalau Naval bilang X, kita ambil 1 turunan untuk konten:" |

| ID | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|

| S-2 | Specific number + duration | "5 hal yang kami pelajari dari 100 carousel di Q1 2026." |
| S-3 | Specific situation | "Kalau LinkedIn kamu cuma post 'congrats team', baca ini." |
| S-4 | Specific objection | "Iya, tapi audiens saya beda — gimana?" |

### Bold Claim Mechanism (forces a stance)

| ID | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| B-1 | Direct claim | "Cold email balik bukan karena copy. Tapi karena timing." |
| B-2 | Negation | "Hashtag ≠ jangkauan. Sudah lama." |
| B-3 | New rule | "Aturan baru content 2026: tiap post harus punya satu KPI." |
| B-4 | Provocative inversion | "Brand awareness kalah penting dibanding brand recognition." |

| ID | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|

## Hook Selection — 60-Second Decision

```
1. What is this piece's job?  (TOFU / MOFU / BOFU / Retention)
2. Audience temperature?       (Cold / Warm / Hot)
3. Channel?                    (IG / LinkedIn / X / TikTok / email / blog)
4. Brand archetype?            (Sage / Hero / Outlaw / Caregiver / etc.)
5. Allowed mechanism?          (Filter library above)
6. Pick 3 candidates from filtered set.
7. Run them past the voice profile (companies/brandflow/clients/<client>/voice.md).
8. Pick #1, save #2-3 as variants.
```

If steps 1-4 take you longer than 30 seconds, the brief is incomplete. Escalate to CMO.

|  | Cold (TOFU) | Warm (MOFU) | Hot (BOFU) | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IG** | Curiosity, Bold, Specificity | Pain, Contrast, Story | Authority, Specific Offer | Story, Insider |
| **LinkedIn** | Bold, Contrast, Authority | Curiosity, Specificity | Authority, Specific Receipt | Story, Confession |
| **X / Threads** | Bold, Contrast | Curiosity, Specificity | Specific, Authority | Insider, Bold |
| **TikTok / Reels** | Pain (visual), Curiosity, Bold | Pain, Contrast, Story | Authority, Bold | Story |
| **Email subject** | Curiosity, Specific | Curiosity, Pain | Specific Offer | Personal-feel |

Use as a starting filter, not a cage. Brand archetype overrides if there's a conflict.

## Channel-Specific Hook Constraints

### Instagram caption
- First 1.5 lines visible before "more" — that's your real hook real estate.
- ~125-140 chars before truncation; design hook to land in that window.
- No clickable links in caption; hook payoff lives in the body or "link in bio."

### LinkedIn post
- First 3 lines visible before "see more" — bigger window.
- Hook can be 1-2 lines; second line can intentionally tease the read.
- Line breaks per 1-2 sentences after hook (mobile-friendly).

### Twitter / X
- 280-char hook tweet must stand alone *and* tease the thread.
- Best hook tweets contain a complete thought + a forward-looking gap.

### TikTok / Reels script
- Hook = first 1.5 seconds of audio + visual.
- Visual hook ≠ caption hook. Both needed.
- Loop-friendly close so the algorithm rewards rewatch.

### Email subject
- 35-50 chars optimal (mobile preview).
- Test: read alone — does it earn the open without preview text?
- Avoid all-caps, multiple exclamations (filters).

### Blog title
- 50-60 chars (SEO + SERP CTR).
- Year/specificity often boosts CTR ("2026 Edition", "for B2B Founders").
- Numbers and "How" / "Why" / "What" structures consistently outperform.

## Hook Stress Test (Before Submitting)

1. **Stand-alone read**: hide the body. Does the hook still earn a read?
2. **Skim test**: imagine 30 posts in a feed. Yours is the hook visible. Does it stop?
3. **Voice match**: re-read against `voice.md`. Any phrase off-brand?
4. **Promise check**: does the body deliver what the hook implies? If no, rewrite the hook OR rewrite the body — never bait.
5. **Specificity ladder**: can you push it from L1→L3 (see content/SKILL.md §5)?

## Anti-Patterns Senior Hooks Don't Use

- **Question-no-payoff.** "Pernah merasa stuck?" with no implied "here's the unstuck."
- **Hyperbole hooks.** "GAME-CHANGING TIPS" / "VIRAL HACK" — the second word kills it.
- **Stock openers.** "In a sea of content..." / "In today's fast-paced world..." — invisible.
- **Hook-bait without follow-through.** Body doesn't deliver the implied promise.
- **Over-clever wordplay.** Pun-as-hook ages badly and rarely communicates.
- **All-caps or excessive emoji.** Reads as desperate.
- **Stacking 3 hooks.** Pick one mechanism. Stacking dilutes.
- **Generic-fit-anywhere hooks.** If you can paste it on any brand, it lands on none.

## Per-Client Hook Bank (Onboarding Output)

When a new client onboards, produce a hook bank — 3-5 patterns from the library above, voiced into the client's specific phrasing. Lives at:

```
companies/brandflow/clients/<client>/hook-bank.md
```

Example structure:

```
[CLIENT]   <name>
[ARCHETYPE] Sage

[HOOK BANK]
1. Pattern: A-1 (Specific receipt)
   Voiced:  "Setelah audit 12 brand UMKM bulan ini, satu pattern muncul:"

2. Pattern: S-1 (Specific persona)
   Voiced:  "Buat founder kos-kosan online yang stuck di 100 follower:"

3. Pattern: T-1 (Origin moment)
   Voiced:  "Sebelum kami tahu pakai save metric, semua dashboard kami salah."

[OFF-LIMITS]
- Provocative-Negation patterns (clashes with Sage archetype)
- All-caps hooks (brand voice formal-leaning)
```

This becomes the copywriter's quick-pick board for that client. Saves 80% of hook-thinking time during production.
