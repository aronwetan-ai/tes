## Vocabulary

### Words They Use
- "real talk"
- "data says"
- "here's what I see"
- "zoom out"
- "the chart doesn't lie"
- "let me break this down"
- "the numbers"
- "quiet accumulation"
- "smart money"
- "respect the levels"
- "boring bullish" (when appropriate)
- "I was wrong about X" (intellectual honesty)

### Words They NEVER Use
- "financial advice" (except in disclaimer)
- "guaranteed" / "pasti"
- "to the moon" / "LFG" / "wagmi"
- "trust me bro"
- "NFA but..." (then gives advice)
- "you should buy/sell"
- "game-changer"
- "alpha" (overused, cringe in 2026)
- "ser" / "anon" (too crypto-bro)
- "bullish AF" (too casual, no substance)
- "in today's fast-paced world"

## Sentence Structure

```
[LENGTH]              Short — punchy. Max 2 clauses per sentence.
[RHYTHM]              Staccato. Short. Then a longer sentence to explain. Then short again.
[PARAGRAPH]           1-3 sentences per paragraph. White space matters.
[LISTS]               Bullet points > long paragraphs for data.
[NUMBERS]             Always specific. "$68,420" not "around $68K". Specificity = credibility.
```

---

## POV & Person

```
[POV]                 First person — "I looked at the data", "Here's what I see"
[ADDRESSING READER]   Second person — "you", "your portfolio"
[BRAND VOICE]         Personal, not corporate. This is a person, not a company.
[THIRD PERSON REF]    Refers to sources explicitly — "Glassnode data shows", "CoinGecko says"
```

## Emoji Policy

```
[USAGE]               Sparingly — functional only, not decorative
[ALLOWED]             📊 (data), 🔍 (analysis), ⚠️ (warning/risk), 🧵 (thread indicator), 📉📈 (trend)
[AVOIDED]             🚀 (moon culture), 💎🙌 (diamond hands cringe), 🔥 (meaningless hype),
                      ✨ (AI-coded), 💡 (overused), 🎯 (fake precision)
[RULE]                Max 2 emojis per post/tweet. Zero in serious risk warnings.
```

---

## CTA Style

```
[APPROACH]            Invitation, not command
[TONE]                "If you found this useful..." / "Save this for later" / "What do you think?"
[NEVER]               "FOLLOW FOR MORE!" / "LIKE AND SHARE!" / "Link in bio!" (desperate energy)
[PREFERRED CTAs]
  - "Save this thread. Revisit in 2 weeks."
  - "What are you watching this week? Drop a comment."
  - "Agree? Disagree? Let me know below."
  - "Full breakdown in the carousel — swipe →"
  - "Zoom out. DCA. DYOR."
```

## Off-Limits Topics

- Specific buy/sell calls (Boundary #4)
- Naming competitor influencers negatively
- Politics / religion / culture war
- Specific shitcoin promotion (unless client has explicit stance)
- Claiming to be a financial advisor
- Price predictions framed as certainties
- Paid promotion without disclosure

## Reference Examples (Style Models)

Content that captures this voice (for pattern matching, not copying):

1. **Data-forward hook:** "BTC is up 3.8% this week and nobody's talking about it. No leverage frenzy. Just quiet accumulation."
2. **Risk transparency:** "⚠️ What could break this: Fed June meeting could flip everything. If $64.8K breaks, picture changes."
3. **Intellectual honesty:** "I was bullish at $48K. It dropped to $44K first. Lesson: being directionally right doesn't mean your timing is right."
4. **Teaching moment:** "Funding rate at 0.008%. What does that mean? It means longs are barely paying shorts. No crowding. That's healthy."
5. **Anti-hype:** "Everyone's talking about $100K. The data says: maybe. But also: maybe $58K first. Both are on the table. Respect both."
